1. Scope and interpretation

The complete visualization catalogue contains 15,786,217 DESI DR1 galaxies and quasars. For each eligible object, the implementation stores a signed radial displacement in physical comoving Mpc. The displacement is obtained from a tracer-specific iterative density-field reconstruction and applied along the observed line of sight. The procedure is intended to provide an exploratory visualization of a partially reconstructed real-space coordinate. It is not an object-by-object measurement of peculiar velocity, and the resulting coordinates are not a scientifically approved real-space catalogue.

Redshift intervals are half-open. BGS is reconstructed continuously over \([0.01,0.40)\), followed by LRG, LRG+ELG, ELG and QSO fields through \(z=2.10\). Objects outside a configured interval, or without an accepted field query, retain zero RSD displacement. The separate FoG term is retained from DESI DR1 Gfinder group analysis.

An authoritative tracer-dependent angular and radial selection, veto and completeness assessment has not yet approved these displacements. The numerical and runtime checks below do not establish cosmological accuracy.

2. Source catalogues and selection

The reconstruction uses DESI DR1 iron/LSScats/v1.5 clustering data and matching clustering random catalogues, separated into North and South Galactic Cap regions (NGC and SGC). The BGS field uses BGS_BRIGHT data and randoms over the entire low-redshift interval. This source contains observations below \(z=0.10\), allowing the field to be estimated on both sides of that boundary. Index-0 randoms form the reconstruction and spatial-support estimate; index-1 randoms provide an additional proximity diagnostic.

Rows require finite right ascension, declination, redshift and total weight, a positive total weight, and membership in the field's redshift interval. The applied weight is WEIGHT × WEIGHT_FKP when FKP weighting is present, otherwise WEIGHT. Duplicate TARGETID rows in an input are resolved by greatest total weight, with the first source row retained on ties. Data and random catalogues receive the same field redshift cut.

3. From observed positions to RSD fields

Observed redshift is converted to Planck18 comoving distance in physical Mpc. With right ascension \(\alpha\) and declination \(\delta\), the position and local line-of-sight unit vector are

\[D_C(z)=c\int_0^z\frac{\mathrm dz'}{H_{\rm Planck18}(z')},\qquad \mathbf s=D_C(z)\begin{pmatrix}\cos\delta\cos\alpha\\\cos\delta\sin\alpha\\\sin\delta\end{pmatrix},\qquad \hat{\mathbf n}=\frac{\mathbf s}{|\mathbf s|}.\]

Each of the five tracer intervals is reconstructed independently in NGC and SGC, giving ten regional fields. The implementation uses pyrecon.IterativeFFTReconstruction 1.0.0, selected weighted data and random positions, local line of sight, three iterations, a 1.2 box pad, a nominal 16 \(h^{-1}\) Mpc cell size, and the field parameters in the table. Length settings are converted from \(h^{-1}\) Mpc to physical Mpc using \(L_{\rm Mpc}=L_{h^{-1}{\rm Mpc}}/h_{\rm Planck18}\). The algorithm assigns data and randoms, forms a smoothed density contrast, iterates the reconstruction, then queries its rsd shift field at accepted catalogue positions.

At a linearized interpretive level, \(\delta_m\simeq\delta_g/b\), \(\nabla\!\cdot\!\boldsymbol\Psi\simeq-\delta_m\), and the line-of-sight RSD contribution scales with \(f(\boldsymbol\Psi\!\cdot\!\hat{\mathbf n})\hat{\mathbf n}\). These expressions explain the role of bias and growth rate; the exact iterative estimator and FFT conventions are those of the recorded pyrecon implementation. If \(\mathbf q_{\rm rsd}\) is the returned shift vector, the stored radial value uses

\[\boxed{\Delta r_{\rm RSD}(\mathbf s)=-\mathbf q_{\rm rsd}(\mathbf s)\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}},\qquad [\Delta r_{\rm RSD}]={\rm Mpc}.\]

The sign and numerical conventions for the previously reconstructed fields were compared with computed exact-ID values, and the recorded source, mesh and sign-consistency gates passed. The continuous BGS_BRIGHT field changes the BGS result across \([0.01,0.40)\), so its comparison with a narrower BGS selection is treated as a sensitivity result, not an equivalence test.

4. Assigning displacements across the full catalogue

Every one of the 15,786,217 visualization objects is processed in its exact catalogue order. Its observed redshift assigns one tracer interval. Exact TARGETID correspondence is used where the source reconstruction catalogue contains that object; other visualization objects can receive a field query only when the observed coordinate passes the spatial-support test. A correction from another tracer interval is never substituted merely because a TARGETID appears there.

For each field and region, the full selected index-0 random catalogue is split into training and held-out subsets. The 99th percentile of held-out nearest-training-random distances defines \(R_{99}\). A query is accepted only when its nearest training random is within that radius, it has unique support in NGC or SGC, and its position lies inside the corresponding FFT box:

\[R_{99}^{(g)}=Q_{0.99}\!\left(\min_{\mathbf r\in\mathcal R_{\rm train}^{(g)}}\|\mathbf r_j^{\rm holdout}-\mathbf r\|\right),\qquad A_i^{(g)}=\left[d_i^{(g)}\le R_{99}^{(g)}\right]\land\left[\mathbf s_i\in{\rm FFTbox}^{(g)}\right]\land\left[\neg A_i^{(g')}\right].\]

The expression abbreviates the implemented order: proximity is evaluated for both regions, multiply supported points are excluded, and the uniquely supported position is checked against its FFT box. Full index-0 randoms are used for the support test even when a deterministic subset formed the reconstruction mesh. This is a numerical interpolation-support proxy, not an official survey mask or a guarantee of valid completeness weighting.

The output is one finite Float32 radial RSD value per object in the exact visualization order, grouped into twelve compressed data chunks. Values with \(|\Delta r_{\rm RSD}|>150\) Mpc are rejected; unmatched entries are zero. The BGS_BRIGHT field supplies 6,326,265 nonzero candidate values among 6,643,924 visualization objects in \([0.01,0.40)\). Across all intervals, 14,345,346 objects receive nonzero RSD values. Two additional correction arrays retain aligned [FoG, RSD] pairs for the full galaxy and quasar catalogues used in detailed object rendering.

5. Visualization-engine application

The visualization engine stores FoG and RSD as separate signed radial terms. A single reconstruction control sets both amounts to the same fraction \(a\in[0,1]\). For observed position \(\mathbf s_i\), the intended rendered position is

\[\boxed{\mathbf x_i(a)=\mathbf s_i+a\,\hat{\mathbf n}_i\left(\Delta r_{{\rm FoG},i}+\Delta r_{{\rm RSD},i}\right)}.\]

At \(a=0\), the original observed coordinate is rendered. At \(a=1\), both available terms are applied along the observed ray. Catalogue identity, observed-redshift filtering and source measurements are unchanged. Detailed object rendering and picking use the same paired corrections as the point catalogue. The FoG arrays were checked equal to their previously retained values after decompression; this reconstruction changed the RSD values while preserving that separate layer.

A runtime check loaded all 15,786,217 objects. At full reconstruction, the detailed galaxy and quasar correction arrays completed loading for 14,140,375 and 1,645,842 catalogue members respectively, and the control returned to zero. This checks data availability and control behavior, not the physical fidelity of the displayed coordinate.

The BGS field was made continuous across \(z=0.10\) because a correction applied only above that redshift created a radial occupancy deficit. In adjacent 5 Mpc comoving bins around the former boundary, observed counts are 19,329 and 19,024. Corrected candidate counts are 19,216 and 18,834. This substantially reduces the measured boundary deficit; it is a point-count diagnostic rather than a cosmological goodness-of-fit test.

On 5,343,619 objects corrected in both BGS configurations, the displacement Pearson correlation is 0.8501 and the median absolute difference is 1.363 Mpc. The broader flux-limited BGS_BRIGHT selection therefore changes the inferred field materially, including above \(z=0.10\). The adopted \(b\), \(f\), random stride, smoothing and mesh choices must be tested for this selection.

7. Scientific review requested

  1. Reproduce the tracer and region selections using the authoritative DESI DR1 angular and radial masks, vetoes and completeness rules. Classify all accepted visualization queries, including objects without an exact clustering-data row, against that selection.
  2. Quantify field sensitivity to random stride and realization, weights, bias \(b\), growth rate \(f\), smoothing, nominal cell size and FFT-box limits. Use mocks or an independent reference where available.
  3. Inspect displacement distributions, sign and radial continuity near \(z=0.01\), 0.10, 0.40, 0.80, 1.10, 1.60 and 2.10, and at NGC/SGC borders and survey holes.
  4. Assess whether adding the retained FoG term to this RSD field double-counts any velocity contribution; compare detailed-object and point positions for representative exact IDs.
  5. Review the radial occupancy result and visually inspect the reconstructed transition around \(z=0.10\). Approve scientific use only after the mask and parameter tests pass on the exact output being assessed.

8. Reproducibility record

Reproduction requires the versioned DESI DR1 LSS clustering data and matching randoms, the exact 15,786,217-object visualization catalogue and object identities, Planck18 distances, and an environment with NumPy, SciPy, Astropy and pyrecon 1.0.0. Preserve input source URLs, file sizes and SHA-256 checksums together with all field parameters.

  1. Apply the same finite-value, positive-weight and half-open redshift cuts separately by tracer and region; record row counts and duplicate-ID resolution.
  2. Convert coordinates and length units as specified above; record mesh dimensions, box center and size for each of ten fields.
  3. Assign weighted data and the prescribed deterministic random subset, form the smoothed density contrast, run three iterations, and query the signed radial RSD shift.
  4. Build the full-random p99 support diagnostic, exclude ambiguous NGC/SGC positions, and apply the FFT-box condition. Keep an independent official-mask assessment separate from this proxy.
  5. Map accepted values into all twelve catalogue chunks and the detailed galaxy/quasar arrays; verify finiteness, exact order, counts, compression round-trip and point/detail agreement.
  6. Check the reconstructed radial counts around field boundaries and exercise the visualization control from zero to full reconstruction and back. Bind any scientific approval to checksums of the exact arrays reviewed.
